The scores use original themes, 4/8-bar phrases, section contrasts, controlled voice leading,
and 120/135/150/165/180 BPM tempos. No melodies from other games are copied.
Renderer: js-synthesizer 1.13.0 / FluidSynth 2.4.6 WebAssembly with libsndfile.
Instrument bank: unchanged MS Basic.sf3 / MuseScore_General_HQ v0.2, licensed under MIT.
Official source: musescore/MuseScore, share/sound, commit 37128c5ac8f9af804b63650610952414421828b8.

The SoundFont copyright/acknowledgements/MIT text remain verbatim in those notices.
Per-source MIDI tempo, instruments, notes, hashes and rendering gain: art_sources/audio/manifest.json.
Format: 44100 Hz, signed 16-bit little-endian PCM WAV (stereo music, mono effects).
Music is 32 seconds (menu/ending) or 64 seconds (stages/boss); one warm-up cycle carries sample tails.
Effects use percussion for combat, soft mallets for UI and piano/strings for short result jingles.
Effects have 1.5ms attack protection and 12ms release protection; repeated shot peak is 0.09.
Per-track peak/RMS/loop checks: art_sources/week2/validation.json.
The game plays rendered WAVs; the MIDI renderer and bank are authoring resources.
